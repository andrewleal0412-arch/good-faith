# Good Faith design system

Zillow Rental Manager's structure with Mercury's calm: a white canvas, neutral grays, one navy, and green, amber and red for status only. The tokens live in `src/app/globals.css` (Tailwind 4 `@theme`, with the default palette, type scale, radii and shadows cleared so nothing outside this system can be used). The shared pieces live in `src/components/`.

## Rules

- White background, neutral grays, one brand color. Green, amber and red mean status and nothing else; each status has exactly one color.
- One family (Hanken Grotesk via `next/font`, weights 400, 500 and 600). Tabular figures on every amount.
- No cream, serif, orange, gradients, emojis or em dashes. Short Sentence-case labels.
- No paragraphs on cards. A row carries a title and at most one sub line. Legal detail and citations sit only behind an info icon, a "Why?" link or a dotted-underline label, never printed under every row. Citations are the exact ones in `docs/LEGAL_RULES.md`.
- One hero number per view. Stat tiles and the facts strip raise the cents at 60%. On a phone one strip never mixes precisions: when every cell is a hero (Properties) all drop their cents, otherwise the hero prints full cents like its neighbours (`phoneCents`). The refund and tenant heroes keep full-size cents.
- One primary button per view. A card whose page already has a primary in its header passes `quiet` and shows its buttons as secondary.
- Empty values are words ("Not set", "Not paid", "None"), never a dash.
- The sandbox shows only as the Test mode switch in the top bar (and the plain word "Sandbox" in the tenant page's bar). The switch is an info tip's trigger: a click, a tap or keyboard focus says what it means and that it can't be switched from the page.
- Dates on screen are short ("Oct 1, 2026"); long dates ("October 1, 2026") are for the printed notices and the statement.
- A property's dates are calendar days where it is (its state's zone), and a clock time outside Pacific time carries the zone's short name ("11:30 PM EDT"); Pacific times stay bare. A delivery recorded on a later day reads "Delivered Oct 2, recorded Oct 6" (a "Recorded" row on the delivery card; "(recorded October 6, 2026)" in the statement's and the PDF's sentence). A city on another clock than its state's shows an amber dotted "Time zone" label beside the Rules chip, like "Local rules".
- A status PayPal reported through its webhook carries "From PayPal" under it in `text-xs` `ink-3` (with the moment where there is room, or in the row's tooltip); the bell lists PayPal's reports under their own "From PayPal" heading, with a green, amber or red dot by what happened. A report that needs the landlord (a reversed deposit, a refund made in PayPal waiting for a decision) shows once, under "Needs you", its sub line carrying PayPal's amount and day, and not again under "From PayPal".
- PayPal's own record sits in a collapsed "PayPal's record" section on the refund receipt and in a Payments row's details (a refund, a settlement, a Payout, the deposit invoice). It reads PayPal when opened and lists the fields PayPal returned, each labelled "PayPal's" and in PayPal's own values ("COMPLETED", "3790.00 USD", its times), with "Read from PayPal just now" and a Check again link. Nothing in it comes from the database or the model. No email, name or address reaches the browser (`src/lib/paypal-record.ts`).
- A refund made in PayPal, not from here, waits on the Payments tab in its own card ("Refund made in PayPal"): one row each, the amount and day as the title, "PayPal refund" and the id with copy as the sub line, and two buttons, "Record as this deposit's refund" (the view's primary unless the page has one) and "Not this deposit". What it means sits behind the card's info icon. Other tabs show an amber banner with "Review", the Overview's Next step reads "Review the refund made in PayPal" with its amount and day, and the record header's primary is "Review PayPal refund" until it is decided. In the bell it is "Refund made in PayPal" under Needs you, and once decided it leaves the bell (the decision is on Activity). One marked not this deposit stays in the card as a gray line with the day it was set aside.

PayPal's two-tone monogram (`PayPalMark`) is the only other color, always beside the word PayPal.

**Type, six sizes:** `text-xs` 12/16 (meta, chips, table headers), `text-sm` 13/18 (table cells, buttons), `text-base` 14/20 (body), `text-lg` 16/24 (section and card titles, fact values), `text-xl` 24/32 (page and record titles, stat values), `text-2xl` 32/40 (heroes).

**Radius:** `rounded-xs` 4, `rounded-sm` 6 (chips), `rounded-md` 8 (buttons, inputs), `rounded-lg` 10 (cards). **Shadows:** `shadow-sm` lifts a card 1px; `shadow-md` is for menus, tips and drawers only.

**Spacing:** sidebar 232px, top bar 56px, content gutter 28px (16px on a phone), card padding 20px, table rows 44 to 48px, sections 24px apart.

**Focus:** one indicator for every control: the 2px `brand` outline 2px outside it (`:focus-visible` in `globals.css`), 11.9:1 on white, easing in on the fast duration. Inputs turn their border `brand` with a 3px `brand-50` ring, and the brand border is the indicator. An AG Grid cell or header draws the outline inside itself, a record tab 3px inside itself (its row scrolls sideways and clips anything outside; a tab the keyboard reaches scrolls whole into the row, clear of its chevron), a menu item inside itself on a `surface` fill, a segmented filter's chip 1px outside itself (its row clips at its 3px padding), a file picker's label around itself (its input is hidden), and a chart's bar or point gets AG Charts' own 2px `brand` line. A `brand-100` ring alone is 1.4:1 on white, under WCAG's 3:1, so nothing uses one as its only indicator.

## Motion

Calm, quick and exact, the way Mercury, Linear and Stripe's dashboard move: things arrive and settle, nothing bounces, nothing performs. Motion makes a change easier to follow; it never decorates, never waits, and never stands in for an answer.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--dur-fast` | 120 ms | Hover, press, focus, a tab's underline and words changing color, a menu or a dialog closing, a tip appearing |
| `--dur-enter` | 150 ms | A page's or a tab's content arriving |
| `--dur-base` | 200 ms | Something opening or changing: a dialog, a menu, the backdrop, a chip's new status, a row leaving, a mark settling in |
| `--dur-slow` | 320 ms | A drawer, a row opening to its height, a line filling, a check drawn, a figure beside the hero or a tile's total counting |
| `--dur-moment` | 560 ms | The demo moments: a box traced, the tenants' progress bar filling, a hero amount counting to its new figure |
| `--ease-standard` | `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.2, 1)` | Everything that arrives or changes: it starts at full speed and slows to rest |
| `--ease-exit` | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` | Everything that leaves: it starts gently and speeds away |

The tokens live in `src/app/globals.css` (every `transition-*` utility takes the fast duration and the standard curve unless it names its own) and, for scripts, in `src/components/motion.tsx` (`DUR`, `EASE`).

**Rules**

- **Nothing waits on an animation.** A click answers in the next frame (the skeleton, the tab, the pressed button), and content is readable from its first frame: an arrival starts from a faint copy of itself (40% opacity, 4px low), never from nothing, and is at rest 150 ms later. Navigation never waits for an exit.
- **Only a real change moves.** A part mounts at rest; a page or a tab arriving eases into place (CSS, which plays again when a hidden tab is shown and never on a re-render); a background refresh that changes nothing moves nothing; a value changes visibly only when it changes while the viewer is looking (`useLiveChange`).
- **Nothing replays what the server painted.** A part hydrated from a full page load was on screen before any script ran, so a moment never hides it to play it again: its first state is only marked seen (`usePainted`). Moments play after a click in the browser: a page opened by a link, a tab shown, a refresh after something the viewer did.
- **Money is exact at rest.** A hero amount (owed back, the deductions, Deposits held) counts to a new figure only when it changes in front of the viewer (a deduction edited, a filter picked) and lands on the exact figure; a figure beside the hero and a tile's total count over the slow token, so clicking through filters stays calm. A screen reader has the final figure throughout (`RollMoney`). A money page never counts on its first paint.
- **Never fake latency.** A working state (the typing dots, the lease's reading bar) shows only while the work really happens and is gone the moment the answer arrives. No spinners. Motion never hides an error or a failed state: those appear at once.
- **Once per viewer, when it is seen.** A demo moment plays the first time this browser sees what it marks (an analysis's boxes, a run's steps, a refund just sent, a step completed since the last visit), once a third of it is in view, and never again (`firstSight`, kept in this browser's storage, or for the page's life when storage is blocked). Until then it waits on its first frame, so scrolling to it shows it play rather than shift.
- **Reduced motion turns all of it off**, in CSS (`prefers-reduced-motion` in `globals.css`) and in every script (`motionOff()`): everything is at its final state at once. Print, the statement PDF and the printed notices never animate.
- **Nothing is added to the layout.** An arrival moves by transform and opacity only; a row opening or closing animates its own height so the rows under it move smoothly instead of jumping.

**What moves**

- **Everywhere:** a page's top-level parts and a tab's content ease up 4px from 40% opacity (`--dur-enter`; a page's first three parts 20 ms apart, 60 ms at most); on a property, a link to a section of the tab already shown scrolls to it and the section makes the same arrival where it lands, so the click answers even when the section was already in place (`tenancy/[id]/tab-state.tsx`); a line in the bell for the page already shown is answered the same way, at the section it names or from the page's top (`components/app-shell.tsx`); skeleton bones carry a soft light that passes over them after 0.4 s; buttons darken on hover and sink 2% on a press; rows, list rows and step boxes shift their background; focus rings ease in; a tab's underline and words change color.
- **Lists that change in place** (findings, payments, the activity, the bell, the negotiation's turns): an added row opens to its height and fades in, a removed one closes and fades out; below the fold, or with reduced motion, it simply appears or goes. In the deductions grid a row added while it is seen fades in, a removed row goes at once (AG Grid's own row animation is off, because `ensureDomOrder` keeps the rows in reading order), and a recomputed amount flashes `brand-50`.
- **Status chips** marked `live` (the record header, the negotiation, the refund, the documents' statement row, the deposit invoice) ease their color and width, and their words come up from a faint copy, when the status changes.
- **Drawers, dialogs, menus, the bell and the phone navigation** open and close with matched motion: a drawer slides 32px in from its edge (a sheet rising on a phone), a dialog rises 6px, a menu drops 4px from its trigger (rises when it opens upward), each over a fading backdrop, and leaves faster on the exit curve. The app has no toasts; Copy's check fades in over its icon.
- **The welcome tour** glides its ring and card from one stop to the next over 400 ms on an in-out curve (`glideCurve`: both ends are resting places, so it starts gently rather than at full speed), aimed at where the part will rest once the page's scroll stops, and waits there while the scroll carries the part in (on a phone the scroll outlasts the glide, and aiming at the moving part sent the ring up and back), then follows the part exactly; each stop's words arrive from a faint copy over the slow token (`components/demo-welcome.tsx`).
- **Hero amounts** count to a new value, as above.

**The demo moments** (`docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` says where each shows in the video)

1. **Inspection boxes:** the first time a viewer sees an analysis of a room, once its move-out photo has loaded and is a third in view, each box's outline is drawn around it in turn (140 ms apart, in its own color; a box waiting its turn is empty), its border takes over and its number settles in (`photo-compare.tsx`). A full load of the Move-out tab shows the boxes as the server drew them.
2. **Negotiation:** while an agent is really writing its turn (the server says the run is going, or this page's request for it is open), a typing row with three dots shows on its side; when the turn lands the dots are gone in that frame, the turn opens into the thread where they were, and the app's check mark and the move settle in just after it (`negotiation-thread.tsx`).
3. **Quick read:** the chip drops in under Type and its likelihood bar fills to the read's probability; a new read changes it in place (the old one dims while the next is on its way, and never picks a Type), the bar easing to the new figure and the words coming up from a faint copy, never vanishing and coming back (`finding-form.tsx`).
4. **Steps:** a step completed in front of the viewer, or one this browser hasn't seen done, draws its check and fills the line to the next step once it is in view, one step after another: the close-out agent's steps under "Done by the agent" on the refund review (after Prepare refund, or when the review is opened from the property), the move-in steps, the tenants' timeline and progress bar when the page refreshes after something the tenants did, and in a demo copy the demo guide's step for a beat that completes while the panel is seen (`components/steps.tsx`, `t/[token]/progress.tsx`, `components/demo-guide.tsx`). At rest the line under a completed step is `green-dot` at 45%, the rest `line`.
5. **Refund sent:** right after Send (the first time this browser sees a receipt in the ten minutes after the refund went), in about 600 ms: the green mark scales in from 88% and its check draws, the amount settles, the receipt's last step ("Sent through PayPal") completes along its timeline, and the rest of the receipt fades in from 200 to 480 ms. No confetti, no bounce (`Moment` in `motion.tsx`, the refund page).

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA, held by `scripts/a11y-check.py` on every landlord page and the tenants' pages at 1280 and 390 px ([docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md](ACCESSIBILITY.md) has what it checks and what it found).

- **Keyboard.** Every control is in the Tab order with the focus outline, and Tab never leaves it hidden: under the shell's top bar the page scrolls a focused control 8px clear of it (`scroll-padding-top`), and a drawer's body keeps it clear of the footer held to its foot. "Skip to content" is the first stop on a landlord page, seen only while it has focus.
- **Menus.** A list of actions (a row's More, Columns, the ledger's filters) is a WAI-ARIA menu: it opens with focus on its first item, the arrow keys, Home and End move, Escape closes it back on its button, Tab closes it and goes on to the next control, and a choice is a `menuitemradio` or `menuitemcheckbox` with `aria-checked` (the check icon shows the same). A grid row's menu, drawn on the body, closes back on its button on Tab too, and there the arrow keys are the grid's. A popover with free content (the bell, Help, the account card) is a small non-modal dialog: its links are in the Tab order, the arrow keys move between them too, and it closes when focus leaves it, focus going on where Tab sent it.
- **Tips.** An info icon, a Why? or a dotted label opens its tip on hover, keyboard focus or a tap; the pointer can move onto the tip (a 150 ms grace covers the gap), Escape closes it whatever opened it without moving focus, and the trigger names it with `aria-describedby`. The trigger is named for what it explains: an info icon by its tip's title ("Deposits held, more information"), a Why? by "Why?" and the title.
- **Targets.** WCAG 2.5.8: a target is 24 by 24 px, or a text link smaller than that with nothing else inside its 24px circle (a row's "View", a card's "Open in Payments"). The tenants' pages, used one-handed on a phone, take no such allowance: every target there is 24px, and a main action is 44px tall and full width. A small control (the info icon, Why?, a dotted label, a link-style button, a disclosure) takes the extra room from padding that negative margins give back, so the line it sits in doesn't move.
- **Grids.** An AG Grid is one Tab stop: the arrow keys move between its headers and cells, Enter acts (a row's details, an input's editor, a charge's drawer), Tab leaves it; while an input is being edited, Tab moves to the next input. A control inside a cell is out of the Tab order, so Enter on its cell presses it (`pressCellControl`): a row's menu opens, a link opens its page, an info tip takes focus and opens; in Insights, Enter on any cell of a tenancy's row opens its property.
- **Names.** Icon-only buttons are named; a button that shows words is named with those words in it ("Date range: All time"). An avatar is a picture (`aria-hidden`), and a mark that means something by color or shape (a checklist's done, missing or not applicable, a timeline's done step) says it in words for a screen reader.
- **Errors.** A red `Notice` is an alert, read out as it appears; a field's note, and its error, are its description (`aria-describedby`). Where an error names one field (the access code, a figure in the deductions grid, the tenants' dispute reasons, name and amount asked back), that field is `aria-invalid`, and on the tenants' forms it takes focus; a server's error that names no field is the alert alone.
- **Phones.** No page scrolls sideways at 390 px, and the tenants' pages and the landlord's main pages don't at 320 either (a desktop window zoomed to 400%); on the tenants' pages nothing is cut off and every amount and date is whole and at least 12px.

## Layouts

- **App pages:** `PageHead` then cards, max width 1208px.
- **A property:** the record header (64px photo, address, status chip, Statement, one primary, overflow menu) over Overview, Move-out, Payments, Documents and Tenants. A next step's link opens another page or one of a tab's cards by name, never a bare tab, so its button answers on the tab it is shown on (`scripts/state-packs-check.mjs` holds that). When nothing is due, the Overview's Next step card says "Nothing due" in plain words, with no button.
- **Grids:** a grid sits in a card that draws its border. Inputs a landlord can change read as small fields inside their cells; a value the code refuses keeps the editor open with the reason in the dark tip under the cell, and the tip says how to leave it: Escape, or a click outside the cell, puts the saved value back. At phone width a grid keeps its first column and the amount, and the rest moves to the row's menu, a drawer or the CSV, never a sideways scroll.
- **Deductions, when none may be kept** (New York after day 14, and Florida once the 30 days for the claim notice ran out; in both, no dispute open): the step's button, "Remove deductions", opens the Deductions card, which carries one line over its grid: a red mark, the step's title with its tip, "None of these deductions can be kept." and one secondary button, "Remove all deductions". It asks once ("Remove all 3 deductions?", with the total) and then every line goes; the line is there only while that is the next step, and the route refuses in any other state.
- **Insights** (`/insights`): the state filter (a segmented control), a four-cell facts strip whose hero is Deposits held, four chart cards in a two-column grid (one on a phone), then the Properties grid grouped by state with a pinned total.
- **Focused flows** (`/tenancy/[id]/refund`): Mercury's Review transfer, a wordmark bar and a centered 560px column, no sidebar. The receipt puts a three-step timeline under the amount (prepared by the agent, approved by you, sent through PayPal, each with its time) in place of a separate "Approved by" row.
- **Tenant page** (`/t/[token]`): the landlord's name in a bar, a 560px column; the amount, one status line and a progress bar first, then Details, Deductions (the thing and what was done, then where and the basis), Timeline, photos, the statement download, a question card and Your rights, each right's title opening its tip. "Prepared with Good Faith" in the footer.
- **Access page** (`/access`): no shell; the wordmark, one line saying what the app is, and a 400px card with the "Access code" field and the one primary, "Enter demo". A wrong code or too many tries shows a `Notice` under the field. The footer line: "PayPal sandbox only. No real money moves. Not legal advice." When the host has no code set, the card is a gray `EmptyState`: "This demo is not configured".
- **Not found and errors:** `EmptyState` inside the shell for landlord pages (`(landlord)/not-found.tsx`, `error.tsx`), on a plain page elsewhere.
- **Waiting on the other side:** a page that shows a sent dispute hears what the other side did without a reload. While the dispute is not settled or withdrawn, the landlord's Negotiation card and the tenants' dispute page each ask a small status route for the dispute's stamp and refresh when it changed (`components/watch.ts`): every 3 seconds while the agents run, every 4 while it waits on a person, every 15 after five quiet minutes, not at all after thirty, never from a hidden tab (or a property tab that hides the card), and at once when the tab is looked at again. A page with no sent dispute, or one that is settled or withdrawn, asks nothing.
- **Loading:** a click on any link in the shell, or Enter in its search, answers in the next frame. Another page shows its own skeleton (`components/skeletons.tsx`: the page's title as text, then its cards, facts and rows as `surface-2` bones at their real sizes) until it arrives; a filter on the same page (Properties' status filter) dims the page; the sidebar marks the page being opened at once. A property's tabs switch in the browser with no request to the server, and Back leaves the property rather than stepping through its tabs (`tenancy/[id]/tab-state.tsx`). A link that names a section of a property (a next step, a banner, Done on the refund review) opens it at that section: once the section's tab is there it is brought to the top and held while the tab settles (the deductions grid draws in the browser), and it is let go the moment the viewer scrolls, clicks or types. On a full page load the shell and the skeleton stream first. A soft light passes over the bones only after 0.4 s, so a quick answer never flickers, and never with reduced motion. No spinners: work that takes a while shows the working dots or, for the lease, a travelling bar.

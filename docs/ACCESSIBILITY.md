# Accessibility

Good Faith is held to WCAG 2.2 AA on every landlord page and on the tenants' pages, from a keyboard, a screen reader's view of the page and a phone. The rules the screens follow are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md#accessibility); this page says how they are checked, what the first check found on 2026-10-07 and what changed, and what is still open.

## The check

```bash
python scripts/a11y-check.py --worktree <a new dir> --shots <dir>
```

It builds the code as it stands in a temporary worktree, serves it on port 3211 with the end-to-end test's stubs on (no Claude call, no PayPal request), makes its own copy of the demo as `scripts/e2e-demo.py` does, audits it, and removes the copy, the server and the worktree. `--serve <built dir>` and `--base <url> --codes <codes.json>` run it against a build already made or served. It records the move-out unit's statement delivery, prepares its refund and asks the tenants' advocate on the way, all stubbed, so the dispute form, the advocate's case and the prepared refund are audited too.

**The bar: no serious or critical axe-core violation anywhere.** axe-core is a pinned devDependency (4.13.0), run with the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules and axe's best practices. Moderate and minor findings are listed under the run (none at the time of writing). The run of 2026-10-07 audited 48 states (every sidebar page, each property's five tabs for all three units, the statements, the refund review, its receipt and the prepared refund, the notices, the lease check with its result, the not-found pages, the access page, the tenants' pages, the dispute form and the advocate's case, the menus, the dialogs, a drawer, the Payments details and the phone navigation), 86 audits across the two widths, and passed 375 checks; the run after the review's fixes, with its new checks, passed 386 of 386.

What axe can't see, checked in a browser:

| Area | What is checked |
|---|---|
| Keyboard | Tab walks every page from its top to its end (50 walks). Each stop shows a focus indicator of at least 3:1 against what is around it, sits on something visible and is never hidden under the top bar, and an outline is painted whole: no box that clips its overflow (a scroll box, `overflow: hidden`) may cut a side to less than half its width. The record tabs and the Payments views, at both widths, are also counted in the screenshot's pixels, all four sides. Every visible control on the page is reached; an AG Grid is one stop and Tab leaves it; Tab reaches each chart. Every info tip and Why? on the pages walked is named for what it explains, never only "More information" or "Why?". |
| Popovers | The bell, Help and the account menu open from the keyboard with focus inside, the arrow keys move, Escape closes them back on their button. Tab past Help's last link goes on to the account button and Shift+Tab out of it to the bell, closing it; the record header's More closes on Tab and focus goes on to the next control, or back to its button with Shift+Tab. An info tip opens on focus, is named by `aria-describedby`, stays open while the pointer moves onto it, and Escape closes it without moving focus. On a phone a tap opens a Why? inside the screen and a second tap closes it. |
| Dialogs | Start over (the confirm dialog), Add finding (a drawer), Record delivery and the phone navigation take focus in, keep Tab inside, close on Escape and give focus back to what opened them. |
| Grids | In the deductions grid the arrow keys move, Enter on a charge's Actions cell opens its menu, Escape gives focus back, Tab leaves. On Payments, Tab reaches the grid, the arrow keys reach a row, Enter opens its details, Escape comes back to the grid, Enter on the Actions cell opens the row's menu, Tab out of the menu closes it back on the row's button, and the arrow keys go on to the next row from there. In Insights, Payments and the deductions grid, Enter on each kind of cell that holds a control presses it: a tenancy's link in Insights opens the property, a group opens or closes, a row's menu opens, a total's info tip opens with focus on it, and on Payments a link's row opens its details, which carry Open property. |
| Errors | On the tenants' dispute form, a ticked charge with no reasons is refused: the red notice is an alert, and the reasons field is `aria-invalid`, described by the notice and focused. |
| Reduced motion | With `prefers-reduced-motion`, nothing is animating after any page loads (82 looks), after a tab is clicked, after a menu, a dialog or the drawer opens, and after the advocate's case lands; the inspection's boxes are drawn at once. |
| Phones | At 390 px no page scrolls sideways. On the tenants' pages nothing is cut off, every amount and date is whole and at least 12 px, every target is at least 24 by 24 px with no spacing allowance, and the main actions are at least 40 px tall and full width. At 320 px (1.4.10 reflow) the tenants' pages and the landlord's main pages still don't scroll sideways. |

## What the first run found, and what changed

The first run, on `78781e3`: axe found two critical violations (the bell's and the account menu's popovers were `role="menu"` holding content that isn't a menu item) and three moderate or minor kinds (no `h1` on the access page, two unnamed `aside` landmarks on the notice pages, an empty header on the tables' actions column). The keyboard walk found more:

- **Focus you could barely see.** Buttons, the info tips, Copy, the account avatar, the photo links and the file pickers drew a 3px `brand-100` ring, 1.3:1 on white; the move-out photos' file picker drew none. Every control now shows the 2px `brand` outline 2px outside it (11.9:1), inputs keep their brand border, and a grid header, a menu item and a chart's bar or point draw their own in brand.
- **Menus from the keyboard.** Opening a menu left focus on its button and the arrow keys did nothing; a row's menu inside a grid was out of reach. A list of actions is now a WAI-ARIA menu (focus on the first item, arrows, Home, End, Escape and Tab back to the button, `menuitemradio` and `menuitemcheckbox` with `aria-checked` for the ledger's filters and columns), a popover with free content is a non-modal dialog, and focus goes back to the button when either closes (Start over's dialog now returns focus to the account button, not the page).
- **Tips.** Escape closed a tip only after a click had pinned it, and moving the pointer from the trigger to the tip closed it. Escape now closes it whatever opened it, a 150 ms grace covers the gap, a tap toggles it on a phone, and each trigger is a 24 px target (14 px before).
- **Grids.** Tab walked every cell (48 stops in a row on Payments, 58 on the Move-out tab) before leaving. Each grid is one stop now, with Enter on the Actions cell for the row's menu.
- **Charts.** AG Charts drew its keyboard focus in its own light blue; it is brand now.
- **Hidden focus.** The sticky top bar could cover a control Tab scrolled to; the page now keeps 64 px clear under it, and a drawer keeps a field clear of its footer.
- **Tenants' pages at 390 px.** Info icons (14 px), Why? (16 px tall), the rights' labels, "Open in browser" and the courts' guide link were under 24 px; "Send to your landlord" was 34 px tall and 161 px wide. All are 24 px or more, the main actions 44 px and full width.
- **Also:** a "Skip to content" link first on every landlord page; placeholders and the icon of an icon-only control in `ink-3` (placeholders were `ink-4`, 2.7:1); the statement checklist's done, missing and not applicable marks said in words; a field's note and error as its description; Check PayPal's answer and Copy's "Copied" read out; the ledger's filter buttons named with the words they show; the tab row's chevron scrolls without smoothing under reduced motion.

## What the review found, and what changed

A review of the round on its own production build found what the check couldn't see then, and the check now covers each:

- **Insights rows out of reach.** With the grid one Tab stop, a tenancy's link in the Property cell left the Tab order, and Enter on its row did nothing, so the keyboard couldn't open a property from Insights. Enter on any cell of a tenancy's row now opens it, and every grid's cells that hold a control are pressed by Enter on the cell (`pressCellControl` in `components/grid.tsx`). The same gap hid the deductions grid's totals' info tips; Enter on a total's line now focuses its tip, which opens it with its text read as the description.
- **Clipped tab outlines.** The record tabs and the Payments views drew the outline 2 px outside each tab, inside a row that scrolls sideways and so clips: the first tab showed one 2 px line. The tab draws it 3 px inside itself now, whole on all four sides at 1280 and 390 px. The new painted check then found the last record tab at 390 px left half under the row's chevron when Tab reached it, its outline cut: a tab the keyboard reaches now scrolls whole into the row, clear of the chevron.
- **A lost Tab.** Tab past the last link of Help, the bell or the account card, or Shift+Tab out of them, closed the popover and put focus back on its button. Focus now goes on where Tab sent it, and Tab out of a menu in the page goes on to the next control. A grid row's menu, drawn on the body, still closes back on its button, and the arrow keys go on through the grid from there instead of opening the menu again.
- **Sixteen "More information"s.** Every info icon was named "More information" and every Why? "Why?". Each is named by its tip's title now ("Deposits held, more information", "Why? The lawful range"), the tooltip trigger no longer says expanded or collapsed, and each Insights chart is a group named by its card's title, since AG Charts names every chart "interactive chart".
- **Errors marked on the field.** The tenants' dispute form announced a refused value but marked no field; the field it names is now `aria-invalid`, described by the error and focused (reasons, name, the amount asked back).
- **Example figures.** The deduction form's number fields lost their example placeholders ("1800", "3", "50", "6", "10"): in `ink-3` they read as values already typed while the form asked for them.

## Open

- **Input borders.** A text field's border (`line`, #E6E8EE) is 1.2:1 against white. WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 of the visual boundary that identifies a field, and the next gray in the palette that clears it is `ink-3` (#64697B, 5.3:1), which would change the look of every form. Left as it is for Andrew to decide; the focused field's brand border is 11.9:1.
- **Small text links on the landlord's pages** ("View", "Open PDF", "Open in Payments", a property's address in a table) are 16 to 20 px tall. They pass WCAG 2.5.8 on spacing (nothing else within their 24 px circle), which axe checks; the run lists them.
- **Not covered:** a real screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA) was not used, only axe and the browser's accessibility tree; the itemized statement's PDF is not a tagged PDF (pdf-lib writes none); the Insights charts are keyboard reachable, and their figures are in the grid under them, which serves as their table.

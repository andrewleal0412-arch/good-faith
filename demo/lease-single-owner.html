RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

State of California · Fixed-term lease

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on September 12, 2025 by and between Teddy Buettner, an individual ("Landlord"), and Luke Jones and Lane Forti (together, "Tenant").

1. Premises

Landlord rents to Tenant the house at 1140 E. Juniper Hollow Ln, Orange, CA 92867 ("Premises"), a two-bedroom, one-bath single-family house with an attached one-car garage, together with the appliances listed in the move-in checklist.

2. Term

The term begins on October 1, 2025 and ends on September 30, 2026, after which it continues month to month unless either party gives notice under California law.

3. Rent

Monthly rent is $2,400.00, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent may be paid by PayPal, Zelle or check.

4. Late Charge

If rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $120.00 (5% of the monthly rent), which Landlord may deduct from the security deposit.

5. Security Deposit

Upon signing, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $4,800.00 (two months' rent). Tenant shall also pay a nonrefundable cleaning fee of $300.00, which covers professional cleaning at the end of the tenancy and will not be returned. The security deposit will be returned as required by California Civil Code Section 1950.5, less lawful deductions.

6. Condition of Premises

Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their present condition. Landlord will photograph the Premises before Tenant moves in. Tenant shall return the Premises in the same condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

7. Utilities

Tenant pays electricity, gas, internet and trash. Landlord pays water and sewer.

8. Pets

One dog under 40 pounds is permitted. Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by the pet.

9. Notices

Notices to Landlord: Teddy Buettner, P.O. Box 2214, Orange, CA 92856, (714) 555-0171, teddy.buettner.rentals@example.com.

10. Ownership

Landlord represents that she is an individual who owns this one residential rental property, a single dwelling unit, and no other rental property, and that she manages it herself.

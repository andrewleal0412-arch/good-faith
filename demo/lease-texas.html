RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

State of Texas · Fixed-term lease

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on September 18, 2025 by and between Harbor View Rentals LLC, a limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Camila Reyes and Owen Whitaker (together, "Tenant").

1. Premises

Landlord rents to Tenant the apartment at 2317 Mesquite Bend Dr, Unit 3, Austin, TX 78745 ("Premises"), a two-bedroom, one-bath apartment in a four-unit building, together with the appliances listed in the move-in condition form.

2. Term

The term begins on October 1, 2025 and ends on September 30, 2026, after which it continues month to month. Either party may end the month-to-month tenancy by giving the other at least 30 days' written notice.

3. Rent

Monthly rent is $1,850.00, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent may be paid by PayPal or check.

4. Late Charge

If rent remains unpaid two full days after the date it is due, Tenant shall pay an initial late charge of $90.00, which Landlord may deduct from the security deposit.

5. Security Deposit

Upon signing, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850.00. Tenant shall also pay a nonrefundable pet fee of $300.00 for one dog, which secures against damage caused by the pet and will not be returned. The security deposit will be refunded as required by Subchapter C, Chapter 92 of the Texas Property Code, less lawful deductions, after Tenant surrenders the Premises.

6. Move-Out and Forwarding Address

Tenant shall give Landlord at least 30 days' written notice of surrender. Tenant shall give Landlord a written statement of Tenant's forwarding address for the refund of the security deposit.

7. Condition of Premises

Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their present condition. Landlord will photograph the Premises before Tenant moves in. Tenant shall return the Premises in the same condition, normal wear and tear excepted.

8. Utilities

Tenant pays electricity, internet and gas. Landlord pays water, wastewater and trash.

9. Pets

One dog under 40 pounds is permitted. Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by the pet.

10. Notices

Notices to Landlord: Harbor View Rentals LLC, P.O. Box 4471, Austin, TX 78765, (512) 555-0148, leasing@harborviewrentals.example. Landlord and Tenant have exchanged e-mail about this Lease, and notices under Subchapter C, Chapter 92 of the Texas Property Code may be sent by e-mail.

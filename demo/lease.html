RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

State of California · Fixed-term lease

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on July 18, 2025 by and between Harbor View Rentals LLC, a California limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Maya Torres and Jordan Lee (together, "Tenant").

1. Premises

Landlord rents to Tenant the residence at 482 N. Glassell St, Unit B, Orange, CA 92866 ("Premises"), a two-bedroom apartment, together with the appliances listed in the move-in checklist.

2. Term

The term begins on August 1, 2025 and ends on July 31, 2026, after which it continues month to month unless either party gives notice under California law.

3. Rent

Monthly rent is $2,800.00, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent may be paid by PayPal, Zelle or check.

4. Late Charge

If rent is not received by the 3rd day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to 10% of the monthly rent ($280.00), which Landlord may deduct from the security deposit.

5. Security Deposit

Upon signing, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $5,600.00 (two months' rent). Tenant shall also pay a nonrefundable cleaning fee of $350.00 and a pet deposit of $500.00 for one cat. The security deposit will be returned as required by California Civil Code Section 1950.5, less lawful deductions.

6. Move-Out Cleaning

Upon vacating, Tenant shall pay for professional carpet cleaning of all carpeted areas regardless of condition, and the cost may be deducted from the security deposit.

7. Condition of Premises

Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their present condition. Landlord will photograph the Premises at the start of the tenancy. Tenant shall return the Premises in the same condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

8. Utilities

Tenant pays electricity, gas and internet. Landlord pays water, sewer and trash.

9. Pets

One cat is permitted. Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by the pet.

10. Notices

Notices to Landlord: Harbor View Rentals LLC, 1520 E. Chapman Ave, Orange, CA 92866, (714) 555-0142, leasing@harborviewrentals.example.

11. Ownership

Landlord represents that it owns and manages six residential rental properties in Orange County comprising 22 units.
